Biopsy Differential & Pathology Reconciliation

Alpha 0.6.13 · Objective morphology → working diagnosis → ordered differential → pathology final → resolved diagnosis
Diagnostic Safety Rule
Pre-biopsy rule: a differential such as “AK vs SCC,” “nevus vs melanoma,” or “psoriasis vs atopic dermatitis” must not be promoted to the active definitive diagnosis before pathology finalization. PH preserves the working diagnosis and ordered differential, then reconciles to the final diagnosis when pathology returns.

1. Biopsy Episode

Objective ↔ Differential correlation: PH compares the documented physical exam against the reference morphology for each differential. Reference descriptors never auto-populate the legal Objective exam; they are used only to organize documented findings and identify possible support or gaps.
