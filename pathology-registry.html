Pathology reports (PDF, image, fax, text), clinic notes and .json case lists are all loaded in the main PH app through Pathology Intake → Choose Files. PH files what it recognizes into this registry. A case leaves the open worklist only when it is closed with a reason; closed cases stay in the registry with their full history.
